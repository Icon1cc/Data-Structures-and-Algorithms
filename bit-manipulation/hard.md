## 1. Minimum XOR Sum of Two Arrays

LeetCode:
https://leetcode.com/problems/minimum-xor-sum-of-two-arrays/

Difficulty:
Hard

Pattern:

- Consider whether the solution can reduce memory or support streaming input.
